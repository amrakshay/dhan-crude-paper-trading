The user journey

Click any box to open that screen. Solid boxes are things you do; dashed boxes are things the system does on its own, the way Dhan's risk desk would.
Set up (once)

1Enable the module P1

Strategies & Features → switch on NSE Options Trader. It can't be armed: you place every order yourself.

2Create portfolios P1

Portfolios → New: "Options Book A" ₹10L, "Hedged Writing" ₹5L, and so on. Each one keeps its own cash, positions and P&L.

3Pick the active portfolio P1

The header picker decides which book the next orders go to, as on every other page.

Trade

4Choose an underlying and expiry P1

Search NIFTY, BANKNIFTY or any F&O stock. The chain streams live; only the strikes on screen are subscribed.

5Build a basket P2

Hover a strike, click B or S. Legs collect on the right with lots, Intraday or Carry-forward, and MKT or LMT.

6Review margin and payoff P2

Overall vs final margin and the hedge benefit, charges, payoff at expiry, max profit and loss, breakevens.

7Place → per-leg result P2

Buys go first, then sells. Each leg fills, part-fills or is rejected with its reason, as in Dhan's order book.

Manage

8Watch positions P2

Grouped by underlying and expiry, with live P&L and the SPAN/exposure split for each group.

9Exit P2

Exit one leg or Exit all, which covers the shorts first. Exiting a hedge leg first may trigger a Hedge Break Alert.

10Check funds P2

Available, SPAN, exposure, delivery margin, premium and MTM, per portfolio.

11Review P&L P1

The existing P&L Reports and Trade Notes work unchanged, filtered by portfolio.

The system acts on its own (RMS and expiry)

AIntraday square-off P3

At 15:25 IST every Intraday position is closed with market orders, shorts first. You get an alert.

BMTM loss ≥ 80% P3

When a portfolio's loss reaches 80% of its blocked margin, its option positions are squared off.

CMargin shortfall P3

A new SPAN file can raise margin overnight. If the book is short, the biggest margin consumer is cut first.

DExpiry and delivery P4

Index options cash-settle at intrinsic value. ITM stock options become a share buy or sell; delivery margin ramps from E-4.
