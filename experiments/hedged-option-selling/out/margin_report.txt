  It is NOT real SPAN, and cannot be: NSE's historical par files are
  not archived (404 at every path probed on 2026-09-20), so no
  historical backtest by anyone can use the exchange's actual number.

  What makes it defensible rather than invented is that the price scan
  range is driven by NSE's OWN published daily volatility (the FOVOLT
  file, available 2016-01-04 .. 2026-09-17), and that BOTH LEGS are
  repriced under every scenario -- so the wing's margin relief is
  measured, not assumed.

==============================================================================
1. THE CALIBRATION -- does ELM net a long against a short?
==============================================================================
The NCL FAQ says ELM 'shall be netted at client and prop levels' and
does not say whether a long option at a DIFFERENT strike counts. Both
readings are implemented. This is the single assumption in the study
most likely to be wrong.

  NIFTY 500-wide bull put spread, 1 lot, F=24,500, 30 DTE, sigma 0.0085

  reading              scan risk         ELM      margin  vs naked  vs max loss
  gross                   18,060      36,750      54,810     62.1%        1.76x
  netted                  18,060           0      18,060     20.5%        0.58x

  Brokers report hedged index spreads at Rs 15,000-30,000 per lot and at
  'roughly a quarter to a third of the margin a naked short needs'.
  The NETTED reading lands inside both bands; the gross reading is
  roughly double the top of each. Netted is used, as a CALIBRATION to
  reported figures rather than a reading of the rule.

2. THE MODEL RESPONDS TO VOLATILITY -- which a flat model does not
==============================================================================
A percentage-of-notional margin holds constant through a crisis, when
the real requirement expands exactly as the position is losing. That
error flatters the strategy precisely where it is most dangerous.

   daily sigma  annualised   scan range      margin   vs calm
        0.0050        9.6%        1,225      18,060     1.00x
        0.0085       16.2%        1,225      18,060     1.00x
        0.0120       22.9%        1,225      18,060     1.00x
        0.0200       38.2%        1,470      21,767     1.21x
        0.0300       57.3%        2,205      28,829     1.60x
        0.0500       95.5%        3,675      31,097     1.72x

  NIFTY's typical daily sigma is around 0.0085 (16% annualised), which
  sits BELOW the scan-range floor, so in calm markets the floor binds and
  margin is flat. It only starts to expand above roughly 1.7% daily
  (32% annualised) -- which is a real property of the rule, not a
  modelling shortcut, and it means margin relief arrives late.
