================================================================================================
DIAGONALS -- sell the near expiry, buy further OTM at expiry+N
================================================================================================
  window 2024-07-01 .. 2026-09-17  (549 sessions)
  Short window by necessity: the structure needs 4+ expiries listed and
  liquid, which NIFTY did not have earlier. Everything below rests on
  about two years, which is not many independent observations.

  'cr/maxL' is the credit as a share of maximum loss -- the risk/reward
  the structure is actually offering.
  Capital at risk is the CONSERVATIVE bound (far leg assumed worthless
  at the near expiry), so the diagonal is flattered by nothing here.

================================================================================================
WHAT THIS SAYS
================================================================================================

  risk/reward offered: vertical 6.6% of max loss as credit, diagonal 1.0%.
  The diagonal is paid LESS per unit of capital at risk, because a
  5-delta strike three expiries out is far away, and the strike
  gap IS the maximum loss. Buying further out in TIME does not
  narrow the gap in PRICE -- and the gap is what you can lose.

  return: diagonal beats the vertical (+25.37% vs -42.82% a year)
  tail:   diagonal worse on the worst trade (-29,797 vs -24,188)

  risk-free over this sample: 5.64% a year.
