  base   iron_condor 0.16d wing@0.05d DTE30+-10 [PT50%]

  Read the trade-off, not the maximum. A best cell picked from a grid
  this size is a selection artefact, and on a strategy whose result is
  decided by two trades in sixty it is close to meaningless.

  'R/risk /yr' is the annualised return per rupee-year of MAXIMUM LOSS
  deployed -- the capital actually at risk, and the only figure here
  that can honestly be set beside the risk-free rate (5.64% over this
  sample). 'RoM /yr' is the same thing against MARGIN, which is a
  LEVERAGED number: the exchange collateralises a median of only ~26%
  of a structure's maximum loss, so it flatters every cell by ~4x.
