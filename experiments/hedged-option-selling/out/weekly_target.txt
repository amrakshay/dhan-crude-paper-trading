======================================================================================
THE "+2% OR -2% OF CAPITAL, WEEKLY" TARGET
======================================================================================

0. THE ARITHMETIC, BEFORE ANY BACKTEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  2.0% a week compounds to      180% a year
  1.0% a week compounds to       68% a year
  0.5% a week compounds to       30% a year

  Risk-free over this sample:                      5.64% a year
  Best structure measured here (bull put spread):  26.00% a year
      -- which is about 0.45% a week.

  So the target is roughly 4.4x the best thing in this study and 32x
  the risk-free rate. That gap is not an exit-rule problem. An exit
  rule decides WHEN you take what the market offered; it cannot
  decide WHAT the market offers.

2. WHAT WEEKLY RETURN WAS ACTUALLY AVAILABLE?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per-calendar-week return on capital employed, best structure, the
+/-2% rule applied. This is the distribution the target has to come
from.

  470 calendar weeks with a completed trade
  mean weekly return on capital at risk : -1.56%
  median                                 : +2.21%
  weeks reaching +2% or better           : 240 (51%)
  weeks at -2% or worse                  : 173 (37%)
  worst week                             : -100.19%

  For a 2%-a-week floor to be met, essentially EVERY week would
  have to clear +2%. The observed share is above.

======================================================================================
WHAT THIS SAYS
======================================================================================

1. THE -2% STOP NEVER DELIVERED -2%. It fired 428 times and
   realised a loss worse than -2.5% on 426 of them (100%).
   Mean realised loss when the stop fired: -9.79%.
   Worst: -48.63%.

   A stop is an instruction, not a guarantee. The position is
   marked at the close, so by the time the mark crosses -2% the
   next tradeable price is already far below it -- and at ~7 DTE
   gamma makes that gap enormous. The rule as written asks for a
   2% loss and delivers a 10% one.

2. CUTTING WINNERS AT +2% WHILE LOSERS RUN TO -10% IS BACKWARDS.
   The +/-2% rule turned a -3.31%/yr strategy into a -67.75%/yr one.
   The target fires often and small; the stop fires less often and
   large. That is the wrong way round for a premium seller, whose
   payoff is already negatively skewed.

3. THE STOP ALONE, HOLDING WINNERS, WAS THE BEST VARIANT TESTED
   (+8.65%/yr, worst trade -11,150 -- the smallest of any variant).
   Stopping losers is defensible; capping winners at 2% is not.

4. THE TARGET WAS AVAILABLE ABOUT HALF THE TIME. 51% of weeks reached
   +2%; 37% were at -2% or worse. A 2%-a-week FLOOR needs essentially
   every week to clear it.

5. NOTE THE MEAN AND THE MEDIAN. Median weekly return +2.21%, mean
   -1.56%. The median week hits the target and the average week loses
   money. That gap IS the strategy's shape, and it is why a rule
   designed around the typical week misprices the book.
