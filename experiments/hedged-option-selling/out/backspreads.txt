BACKSPREADS -- buy more wings than you sell
================================================================================================
  2016-01-01 .. 2026-09-17   bull put spread, 16d short / 5d wing, ~30 DTE
  ratio = long wings per short leg. 1.0 is the ordinary vertical.

  NOTE ON A BUG FOUND WHILE BUILDING THIS. The profit target used to be
  a fraction of `max_profit`. For a vertical that IS the credit, but a
  backspread's max_profit is its payoff at spot near zero -- Rs 840,000
  against a Rs 1,600 credit -- so the target was unreachable and every
  backspread silently ran to expiry while the vertical took profit at
  50%. The first version of this table compared two different
  management rules. The target is now a fraction of the CREDIT, which
  is identical for every non-backspread structure and leaves all other
  results in this study unchanged.
